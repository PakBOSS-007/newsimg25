# Sum up 25 November's world news in one image

An entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 25 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

## The image

A newspaper front page, `entry.png` (1024×1024), summing up Wednesday
25 November 2026 in six stories. The date is named twice, in the
masthead and in the footer, so a viewer who missed the day can tell
which day the page sums up. Each story carries a coloured category
label, a bold serif headline and a one-line summary, so the page
reads at a glance and at about 300 pixels across.

## The six stories

Every story below happened, or was first reported, on 25 November
2026, and each was cross-checked against at least two independent
roundups of that day's news before it was put on the page.

1. **Two oil tankers hit mines and catch fire in the Strait of
   Hormuz** (lead). Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said
   the vessels struck naval mines and exploded as they tried to
   cross the world's busiest oil waterway. Reported by Just
   Security's Early Edition, by the day's video roundup and by the
   Open Chronicle morning brief.
2. **Houthi drone attack on a Riyadh tower injures 11.** The strike
   hit a tower in the capital's King Abdullah Financial District;
   the Houthis also said they attacked the Ras Tanura refinery.
   Reported by Just Security and by the day's video roundup.
3. **US jury convicts Huawei of stealing trade secrets.** A federal
   jury in Brooklyn found Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts,
   including bank fraud and conspiracy to steal trade secrets, and
   cleared it of the racketeering charge. Reported by Just
   Security, by the day's video roundup and by Principia.
4. **Ukrainian drones set two Russian oil refineries ablaze.**
   Drones hit the Syzran and Kuibyshev refineries in Russia's
   Samara region; one person was killed. Reported by the day's
   video roundup and by the Open Chronicle morning brief.
5. **Australia and Solomon Islands sign treaty barring foreign
   bases.** The treaty precludes the establishment of any foreign
   military or security base on the Solomon Islands. Reported by
   Just Security, by the day's video roundup and by the Open
   Chronicle morning brief.
6. **China launches Pakistan's first astronaut into orbit.**
   Shenzhou-24 docked with the Tiangong station about three and a
   half hours after launch, carrying Pakistan's first astronaut.
   Reported by the day's video roundup and by the Open Chronicle
   morning brief.

Stories left out on purpose: the COP31 collapse (already the previous
day's news, covered in the 24 November entry), the ICJ ruling on
Myanmar (reported as "last week", so its date was not certain
enough), and the Banten factory fire (a 24 November story).

## How it is built

`draw_poster.py` renders `entry.png` with PIL and the Liberation
fonts (Liberation Serif Bold for the masthead and headlines,
Liberation Sans for the labels, summaries and footer). The layout is
a newspaper front page: a navy masthead with the paper's name and
the date in gold, a lead story, a rule, then five more stories
separated by hairlines, and a footer that repeats the date. The
colours are flat and the text is drawn, never generated by an image
model, so no headline is garbled.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`; the copy is byte-identical on every run, with
no network and no model calls.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Render the poster again from the script:

```sh
python3 draw_poster.py
```

## Verification

- `entry.png` is 1024×1024 pixels and 114,088 bytes, inside the
  1 MiB and 1024×1024 limits.
- `main.ts` run twice writes byte-identical output, and the output
  matches the `entry.png` in this repository.
- The poster was read back after rendering: every headline and
  summary is legible, nothing overflows the margins, and the date
  appears in the masthead and the footer.
